# Workflow: Course Withdrawal

## Confirmed requirements

- Planned withdrawal notice is at least 30 days.
- Preserve purchase and learning history.
- Record affected learners, reason, decision date, notice date, scheduled withdrawal date and notification delivery status.

## Recommended baseline

1. Authorized operator records withdrawal decision and reason.
2. Course enters Withdrawal Scheduled.
3. Stop new sales, subject to approved business policy.
4. Identify all affected learners with purchase/entitlement records.
5. Send notice and record delivery attempts/status.
6. Keep course and progress records intact.
7. On scheduled date, mark course Withdrawn.
8. Apply the approved access outcome to existing purchasers.
9. Preserve audit and support history.

## Critical unresolved policy

Do existing purchasers retain access after withdrawal?

Recommended: preserve access where the platform retains legal rights to provide the content. If rights require removal, use a documented exception process and define learner remedy. This recommendation is not final until approved.

## Failure cases

- Notification provider unavailable.
- Some learners cannot be reached.
- Withdrawal date changes.
- Legal takedown requires faster action.
- Course has active learners near completion.
- Instructor/rights holder disputes continued distribution.
- Course has already been purchased but payment is later reversed.

Do not silently delete course records, certificates, purchase history or learning progress.

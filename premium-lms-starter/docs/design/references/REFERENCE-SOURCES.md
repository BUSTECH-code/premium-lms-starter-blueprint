# Premium LMS Visual Reference Sources

This file is the curated visual reference index for the initial LMS. It intentionally links to third-party examples rather than copying their image files into this repository. Review each source's terms before downloading, modifying, or redistributing screenshots. Use screenshots as inspiration, not as assets to reproduce.

## How the agent must use this index

1. Inspect the image previews and open the source pages where possible.
2. Record the specific design lessons relevant to our product.
3. Do not copy a reference's brand, exact composition, text, illustrations, or components wholesale.
4. Do not infer product functionality from a static screenshot.
5. If the agent cannot access an image, it must say so; it must not claim to have inspected it.
6. Product-owner-selected local screenshots may be added beside this file after confirming permission to store them.

## 1. Public academy homepage — editorial hero and clear proposition

**Reference:** Augment Business School landing page, shown in a Circle customer story.

![Augment Business School landing page](https://circle.so/_next/image?q=75&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.builder.io%2Fapi%2Fv1%2Fimage%2Fassets%252Fd5e2d72a033944e1a916c5c34902a1ff%252F9d12d855531e431b8b184d1af85862b0&w=3840)

Source: https://circle.so/customer-stories/augment-business-school

**Study:** Strong hero hierarchy, restrained navigation, concise proposition, clear primary action, use of imagery to support the message. Do not copy its neon accent or dark palette as an assumed LMS brand decision.

## 2. Course catalogue — course discovery and metadata hierarchy

**Reference:** E-learning course catalogue concept.

![Course catalogue concept](https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/16001143/file/original-63d14dc8803d708fec7792799c36404c.png?resize=%7Bwidth%7Dx%7Bheight%7D&vertical=center)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/24660545-Course-Purchase-Checkout-UI

**Study:** Course image/title relationship, scan-friendly metadata, clear price and purchase action. The example is only a starting point; avoid copying its repetitive card grid as the entire catalogue design.

## 3. Course details — curriculum and purchase information

**Reference:** Learning platform course detail concept.

![Course detail concept](https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/6644233/file/original-af53f8e68efb614f6dcb0aa757b39e4c.png?resize=2400x1800&vertical=center)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/21332311-Course-page-for-a-learning-platform

**Study:** Curriculum grouping, lesson duration, course summary and purchase information. Improve the information hierarchy and ensure all displayed claims come from real course data.

## 4. Student dashboard — continue learning and progress

**Reference:** Academic dashboard concept.

![Academic dashboard concept](https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/13609432/file/original-39825ee49f73a425c9b62624629c6b88.jpg?resize=752x&vertical=center)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/23836759-Academic-Dashboard

**Study:** Navigation clarity, continue-learning priority, course progress and resource access. Do not copy decorative statistics or add metrics unless the product has reliable data for them.

## 5. Video learning — lesson player and curriculum context

**Reference:** Online video-course lesson interface.

![Video lesson interface](https://cdn.dribbble.com/users/2099699/screenshots/7496701/video_lessons_site_4x.png)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/7496701-Online-learning-platform-video-courses

**Study:** Video prominence, relationship between lesson and course navigation, lesson context and supporting files. The final design must also handle small screens, low bandwidth, playback errors and offline availability.

## 6. Materials library — supporting resources in lesson context

**Reference:** Use the supporting-file area in the video lesson example above as a starting point, not a complete library design.

![Video lesson with supporting files](https://cdn.dribbble.com/users/2099699/screenshots/7496701/video_lessons_site_4x.png)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/7496701-Online-learning-platform-video-courses

**Study:** Clear separation of lesson content and downloadable resources. The LMS should design a more complete materials library with file type, size, download state, offline status and course/module context.
